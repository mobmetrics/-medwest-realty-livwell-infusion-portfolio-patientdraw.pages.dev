What the data says

Monterey draws nearly seven times Burbank's patients — and the reason is competition, not demographics.

Monterey is the only California LivWell with no competing infusion clinic inside 30 miles; the nearest is 45.6 miles away in San Jose. Burbank has thirteen inside 30 miles, one of them just 0.6 miles away, plus five other LivWell clinics drawing on the same area. Household incomes and patient travel distances in the two markets are broadly comparable.

Monterey vs. Burbank scorecard

Burbank sits in the bigger, better-connected market. Monterey still draws several times the patients — and the reason is not distance or demographics.

Why Monterey outperforms

Monterey wins by having its region to itself. The gap is not about how far patients travel — Burbank's patients simply have more places to choose from.

What actually differs between the two locations — and why it isn't distance or demographics.

Market saturation, not distance

Monterey's middle patient lives 3.3 miles from the clinic; Burbank's lives 5.5 miles away. Neither clinic is asking for a long trip, and the shorter trip belongs to Monterey — so distance is not what is holding Burbank back.

But how far each clinic reaches is a difference

The middle of each clinic’s area looks similar; the edges do not. Nine out of ten of Monterey's patients live within 16.8 miles, and 114 of them share the clinic's own ZIP code. Burbank's outer edge stops at 13.3 miles — roughly where its nearest LivWell neighbours sit — and only 5 patients share its ZIP code. Monterey owns a corridor; Burbank owns neither a core nor a periphery.

Captive geography vs. abundant alternatives

Monterey's clinic is one of only a handful of options across an entire region — just 4 hospitals cover the whole Monterey Peninsula and Salinas Valley, and the southernmost of those, in King City, is nearly an hour away. Burbank sits inside the Los Angeles basin, surrounded by 6+ hospitals in the immediate area and dozens more (Kaiser, Cedars-Sinai, UCLA-affiliated systems) a short drive away. Patients there have far less reason to stay loyal to any single location.

Highway access cuts both ways

Burbank benefits from excellent freeway access (I-5, SR-134, SR-170) — but that same connectivity makes it just as easy for patients to drive to a competing provider. Monterey's more limited access (CA-1, CA-68, no direct Interstate) works in the location's favor once patients are already in the region: there's simply less competition to drive to.

LivWell may be competing with itself

Burbank has five other LivWell clinics within 30 miles, the nearest 6.4 miles away, and draws only 9% of its patients from Burbank itself. Monterey has two other LivWell clinics inside 30 miles, the nearest 24.1 miles out, and draws 19% from its own city. Across the portfolio, the two clinics LivWell has already graded land exactly where the theory predicts: Monterey is 2nd most isolated of 29, Burbank 3rd least.

Demographics are a smaller factor than expected

Monterey County (median household income ~$97,200, median age 35.5) and Burbank (~$85,500–97,000 depending on source, median age 40.1) are broadly comparable. This isn't primarily an income or age story — it's a competitive-density story.

An affluent, part-time population

Twelve Monterey-area patients list permanent addresses outside California — Texas, Georgia, Arizona, Tennessee, New Jersey, Pennsylvania and Alaska among them — consistent with Carmel and Pebble Beach's profile as a wealthy second-home market. Only one such address appears in the Burbank list.

Building and market context

Monterey Peninsula's medical office stock is boutique and supply-constrained. Burbank sits in the Tri-Cities office submarket (Burbank/Glendale/Pasadena), running roughly 24–26% vacancy across asset classes — a market that makes it easy for competing providers and space to enter.

Competitors sharpen the picture — and complicate it

Adding the three main California competitors — Infusion For Health (46 sites), IVX Health (12) and Clinivoy (7) — makes Monterey look stronger and Temecula weaker. Monterey is the only California LivWell with no competitor inside 30 miles; the nearest is 45.6 miles away in San Jose. Burbank has thirteen inside 30 miles and one just 0.6 miles away. Counting other LivWell clinics and competitors together, Monterey is the least crowded of the 21 California locations and Burbank the 16th.

The untested prediction, with a caveat

Temecula sits further from another LivWell than any other location — 32.8 miles to the nearest, none within 30 — and nobody has graded it. But it is not alone in its market: an Infusion For Health clinic sits 0.6 miles away on Ynez Road, and four competing clinics fall inside 30 miles. So Temecula tests a narrower claim than it first appears. If distance from other LivWell clinics alone drives performance, it should look like Monterey. If total clinic density is what matters, it shouldn't — and Monterey's zero-competitor position becomes the better explanation of why it wins. Either way the answer sits in LivWell's own books, and either way we learn which of the two mechanisms is real.
